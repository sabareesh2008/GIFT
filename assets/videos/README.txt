CINEMATIC INTRO VIDEO
1. Export your intro as an MP4 (H.264 recommended).
2. Copy it into this folder.
3. Rename it to: placeholder-intro.mp4
4. The intro page loads it automatically, and when the video reaches its natural end, the site fades into HER LITTLE UNIVERSE.
5. The visitor can still choose Skip video or Enter your universe.

Exact path:
assets/videos/placeholder-intro.mp4

If you want the clip to play with its original audio, remove the `muted` attribute from the <video> tag in index.html. Note: browsers may block autoplay when sound is enabled; the visitor may need to press play. The default muted autoplay is the most reliable option.
